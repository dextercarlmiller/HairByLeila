Meet Leila

Welcome! I'm Leila — an independent hairstylist with nearly two decades of experience right here in Fort Wayne, Indiana. I work out of the beautiful Lotus Spa on Speedway Drive, where every client gets my full, undivided attention.

Whether you're dreaming of a sun-kissed balayage, a bold color transformation, a fresh cut, or silky extensions — I bring both artistry and heart to every chair. My goal is simple: make you feel beautiful, confident, and heard.

I'm proud to be Fort Wayne's highest-rated independent stylist, with hundreds of 5-star reviews from clients who keep coming back. I'd love to add you to that family.
