PHOTO PLACEHOLDERS — Hair By Leila Website
==========================================

Hi Leila! Here's a guide to every place on the site where a photo should go.
Simply replace the placeholder elements with <img> tags pointing to your image files.

Place all your photos in this folder: assets/images/

------------------------------------------------------------
1. index.html — Hero Section
   What: A full-width background image of your salon, a gorgeous hair transformation,
         or a professional shot of you at work.
   Where in code: <!-- PHOTO PLACEHOLDER: Full-width hero background image -->
   How to use: Add a CSS background-image on .hero, e.g.:
     background-image: url('../assets/images/hero-bg.jpg');
     background-size: cover; background-position: center;

2. index.html — Intro Section (Meet Leila)
   What: A warm, professional portrait of you — smiling, in your element.
   Where in code: <div class="intro__image-placeholder">
   How to use: Replace the div with:
     <img src="assets/images/leila-portrait.jpg" alt="Leila, Fort Wayne hairstylist" />

3. index.html — Instagram Teaser Grid (6 photos)
   What: Six of your best work photos — balayage results, color transformations, cuts, etc.
   Where in code: <div class="ig-placeholder">📸</div> (6 of these)
   How to use: Replace each div with:
     <img src="assets/images/work-1.jpg" alt="Balayage by Hair By Leila" />
     (number them: work-1.jpg through work-6.jpg, or use descriptive names)

------------------------------------------------------------
4. services.html — CTA Background
   What: An atmospheric photo of you working in your salon — candid and warm.
   Where in code: <!-- PHOTO PLACEHOLDER: Background image of Leila working in her salon -->
   How to use: Add a CSS background-image on .services-cta or .services-cta__box.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. about.html — Portrait (large)
   What: Your best professional photo — larger format, 3:4 or 4:5 ratio looks great.
   Where in code: <div class="about-photo__placeholder">
   How to use: Replace the entire div with:
     <img src="assets/images/leila-portrait-large.jpg"
          alt="Leila — Fort Wayne independent hairstylist"
          style="border-radius:12px; width:100%; box-shadow:var(--shadow);" />

6. about.html — Gallery Teaser
   What: A collage or mosaic of your best before-and-after transformations.
         Could also be a single dramatic before/after side-by-side.
   Where in code: <div class="gallery-teaser__placeholder">
   How to use: Replace the div with a grid of <img> tags, or a single large image.

------------------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDED IMAGE SIZES:
  Hero background:    1920 × 1080px minimum (JPG, compressed to ~200–400KB)
  Leila portrait:     800 × 1000px (JPG, ~100–200KB)
  Instagram grid:     600 × 600px each (JPG, ~80–120KB each)
  Gallery teaser:     1200 × 600px (JPG, ~150–250KB)

TIPS:
  - Use descriptive filenames: leila-balayage-result.jpg beats IMG_1234.jpg
  - Compress images before uploading (use squoosh.app for free)
  - Add alt text to every <img> for accessibility and SEO
  - WebP format will load faster than JPG if your photos are large

If you have questions, reach out to your web developer. You've got this! 💛
