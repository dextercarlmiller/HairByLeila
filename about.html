Hi, I'm Leila.

I've been doing hair for nearly two decades — and I still love every single day of it. What started as a passion became a career, and what became a career became a calling. There is nothing more rewarding to me than watching a client see themselves in the mirror and light up.

I'm an independent stylist, which means I work for myself — and that means I work entirely for you. No quota. No rushing. No cookie-cutter services. When you're in my chair, you have my full attention, my honest advice, and my genuine care for the health of your hair.

I'm based inside the beautiful Lotus Spa on Speedway Drive here in Fort Wayne, Indiana. It's a warm, welcoming environment that reflects the experience I want every client to have — calm, comfortable, and completely personal.

Over the years I've become known especially for color work — balayage, highlights, and color correction are where I really shine. But I love it all: a great cut, a silky blowout, extensions that blend perfectly, a keratin treatment that changes someone's morning routine forever.

I'm proud to be recognized as Fort Wayne's highest-rated independent hairstylist, with hundreds of glowing 5-star reviews. But what I'm most proud of is the relationships I've built — clients who have been coming to me for years, who trust me completely, and who leave feeling their absolute best.

My Philosophy

What you can always expect when you sit in my chair.

💛

Personalized Service

Every client is different. Your hair, your face shape, your lifestyle, your goals — I take all of it into account before I ever pick up a brush or scissors. There's no one-size-fits-all here.

🌿

Hair Health First

Beautiful results should never come at the expense of your hair's health. I use quality products and honest techniques that keep your hair strong, vibrant, and feeling great.

🤝

Honest Consultation

I'll tell you what's possible, what's realistic, and what's best for your hair — even if it's not exactly what you walked in asking for. That's what real expertise looks like.

⭐

Client Satisfaction

You leave happy — full stop. Hundreds of 5-star reviews don't happen by accident. They happen because I genuinely care about every person who sits in my chair.
